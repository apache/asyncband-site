# Community Report & Maturity Assessment

> **Updated at: 2026-11-22.** This page is a point-in-time community report and self-assessment prepared during the graduation readiness review of Apache Asyncband (Incubating) ([apache/asyncband#422](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/422)) rather than a continuously updated dashboard. After graduation, individual counts and links may become historical; refer to the [Community](/community/) and [Downloads](/downloads/) pages and the [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/apache/asyncband) for current project state.

## Incubation community summary

Apache Asyncband (Incubating) entered the Apache Incubator on **2026-08-18** following the [proposal discussion](https://lists.apache.org/thread/f31qd3jm3odomjwy3lqkk21coyqsr9xs) and [acceptance vote](https://lists.apache.org/thread/3txlwbp683p9dswwypcdqqq1863khgj5), continuing the Rust concurrency library formerly published as `mea`.

* **Naming and IP clearance**: VP, Brand Management approved the "Apache Asyncband" name in [PODLINGNAMESEARCH-257](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/PODLINGNAMESEARCH-257). Initial contributions are covered through contributor ICLAs along the CLA-based path in the [Podling IP Clearance guide](https://incubator.apache.org/guides/ip_clearance.html), recorded in [r1940210](https://svn.apache.org/viewvc?view=revision&revision=1940210) on the [Incubator status page](https://incubator.apache.org/projects/asyncband.html).
* **ASF releases and shared release management**: The community has published **four** ASF-approved source releases managed by **four distinct release managers**:
  * **0.7.2** (2026-09-10, RM: Zili Chen / `tison`): [#285](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/285), [dev@ result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/v95sz78g91k2v3s5tbjqkmmhxbx8zzlt), [IPMC result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/crxnfmk0r6xnm6q71f81zfvb33dmtgo9).
  * **0.7.3** (2026-09-29, RM: Haixu Ouyang / `orthur`): [#327](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/327), [dev@ result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/oq439gtwz9o3ml208fw6glskzk8rcyrr), [IPMC result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/q8krpkv0qvfqkvhcls2hnvoov4pd0v0t), [announcement](https://lists.apache.org/thread/8f6kyd5pxfzshy3ofqj1vylnc6xfl3jh).
  * **0.7.4** (2026-10-25, RM: Bohao Zhang / `iqwqi`): [#369](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/369), [dev@ result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/vfgx30gxbrdpbqmp097osqm44mnhtoxm), [IPMC result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/06tyxy37bjzbkojsh2l9mhnhdw59x37b), [announcement](https://lists.apache.org/thread/8w8whrndgrd66joq0qzyqkvh0wcofmnl).
  * **0.8.0** (2026-11-16, RM: Yijun Zhao / `ariesdevil`): [#417](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/417), [dev@ result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/30sgo1g35x4vszlpr69nbl7m5o0xc1k1), [IPMC result](https://lists.apache.org/thread/g60s9grz0vm8oxhjll35b8t73jw9t2lf), [announcement](https://lists.apache.org/thread/99qo4fjk8psh99h5g3wkwwoh1yv5r49q).
* **Historical non-ASF crates**: Version `0.6.7` (2026-08-13) predates incubation entry as the compatibility point for migrating from `mea` ([`MIGRATE.md`](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/blob/main/MIGRATE.md)). Early transitional crates `0.7.0` ([cancelled RC vote](https://lists.apache.org/thread/89hpy1rndvo7y8gb0o5ckc4g8qfld2sd)) and `0.7.1` were not approved ASF releases and have both been yanked on [crates.io](https://crates.io/crates/asyncband/versions).
* **Community growth and independence**: During incubation, the PPMC elected **three** new committers and PPMC members: Bohao Zhang (`iqwqi`, [welcome](https://lists.apache.org/thread/wjh3yhkzmy3j8z5k6vxk9v25w9bmo1sd)), Yijun Zhao (`ariesdevil`, [welcome](https://lists.apache.org/thread/vc2n9zlb7rxx7mr43z7r93ylt9xgjszn)), and Jinlin Zhu (`nanami`, recorded in [r1940660](https://svn.apache.org/viewvc?view=revision&revision=1940660)). The 10-member PPMC and committer roster spans multiple legally independent employers, open-source communities, and individual contributors, with no single organization holding control.
* **Shared maintenance and ongoing focus**: Because Asyncband originated as an individual open-source library (`mea`) before entering the Incubator, all-time commit counts remain weighted toward the original author (`tison`). During incubation, however, release management (the three most recent releases), new primitive design and implementation (including `Phaser` [#250](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/250), bounded MPMC `broadcast` [#253](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/253), competing `mpmc` and `spmc` queues [#265](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/265) and [#305](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/305), `AutoResetEvent` [#310](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/310), and `UpgradableRwLock` [#366](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/pull/366)), code reviews, and website and blog engineering ([apache/asyncband-site#44](https://github.com/apache/asyncband-site/pull/44)) have been shared across multiple committers and external contributors. Continuing to broaden review and subsystem ownership remains an ongoing community goal alongside the pre-1.0 roadmap ([#445](https://github.com/apache/asyncband/issues/445)).
